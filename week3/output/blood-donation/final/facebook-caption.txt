Give blood and save lives. It really is that simple ❤️

World Assicion warmly invites everyone to our Blood Donation Day on Saturday 14 November 2026 at Royal Road in Curepipe. We will be there from 9:00 to 14:00 and we would love to see you.

Blood helps patients every single day, from people going through surgery to mothers facing complications in childbirth. One donation can help save up to three lives.

Planning to come? Have a good meal before you donate and drink plenty of water so you feel your best on the day. Bring a friend or the whole family along. Everyone is welcome and there will be goodies to say thank you 🎁

Have a question about whether you can donate? Our team on site will be happy to help.

See you on the 14th!
